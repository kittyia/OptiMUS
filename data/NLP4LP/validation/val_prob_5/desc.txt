A hospital needs to create a weekly schedule for its nurses working the night shift from 12 PM to 8 AM. The demand for nurses during the night shift varies each day of the week. For each day, there is a specific number of nurses required.
Each nurse works for a certain number of consecutive days on the night shift, followed by several days off, ensuring they have a balanced work-rest schedule over the week. The challenge is to determine the minimum number of nurses the hospital needs to hire to meet the varying daily demands for the night shift throughout the week.
