A audio company makes two types of headphones: wired headphones and wireless headphones. Two different teams make each type of headphone. The wired team can make at most MaxWiredProduction wired headphones per day and the wireless team can make at most MaxWirelessProduction wireless headphones per day. Both teams require use of a shared audio testing machine, and this machine can be used to make a maximum of MaxTestingCapacity headphones per day. The profit per wired headphone is ProfitWired and the profit per wireless headphone is ProfitWireless. How many of each headphone should be made to maximize profit?
