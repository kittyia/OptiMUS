A lighting company makes two types of lamps: desk-lamps and night-lamps. There is an expected demand of at least DemandDeskLamps desk-lamps and DemandNightLamps night-lamps each day. However, due to the size of their factory, they can make at most MaxDeskLamps desk-lamps and MaxNightLamps night-lamps per day. To satisfy a contract, a minimum of MinTotalLamps lamps must be made each day. The profit per desk-lamp sold is ProfitDeskLamp and the profit per night-lamp sold is ProfitNightLamp. The objective is to determine the number of desk-lamps and night-lamps to maximize profit while meeting demand and contract requirements.
