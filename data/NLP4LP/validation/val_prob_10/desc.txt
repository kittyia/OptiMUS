We have a random variable Z that takes values 0, 1, ..., K with probabilities p_0, p_1, ..., p_K, respectively. We are given the values of the first two moments:

E[Z] = \sum_{k=0}^{K} k p_k
E[Z^2] = \sum_{k=0}^{K} k^2 p_k

We would like to obtain a lower bound on the value of the fourth moment:

E[Z^4] = \sum_{k=0}^{K} k^4 p_k

Use linear programming to find a lower bound on E[Z^4].
