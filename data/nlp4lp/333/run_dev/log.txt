- The **non-negativity constraint** is essential for a valid LP formulation. Without it, negative shipments could artificially reduce cost and violate physical meaning.
- No pair of constraints can be combined because they are structurally different (indexed differently and involving different inequality/equality relations).

Therefore, **no redundancy exists**, and **no constraints can be removed or combined**.
