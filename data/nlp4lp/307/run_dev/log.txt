- Refining raw oils into final product (with no loss of weight),

- Initial inventory,
- Monthly purchases,
- Monthly refining decisions,
- Monthly storage levels,

is through a **material balance (flow conservation) constraint**.

### Interpretation of the constraint

For each oil \( i \) and month \( m = 1, \dots, M \):

\[
\text{storage}_{i,m-1} + \text{buy}_{i,m} - \text{refine}_{i,m} = \text{storage}_{i,m}
\]

This ensures:
