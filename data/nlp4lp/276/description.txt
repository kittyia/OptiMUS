Consider a rocket that travels along a straight path. Let x_t, v_t, and a_t be the position, velocity, and acceleration, respectively, of the rocket at time t.
By discretizing time and by taking the time increment to be unity, we obtain an approximate discrete-time model of the form 
    
    x_{t+1} = x_t + v_t
    v_{t+1} = v_t + a_t

We assume that the acceleration a_t is under our control, as it is determined by the rocket thrust. In a rough model, the magnitude |a_t| of the accelera­tion can be assumed to be equal to the rate of fuel consumption at time t. 
Suppose that we have x_0 and v_0 as the initial position and velocity, respectively, of the rocket. We wish the rocket to take off and reach a target position x_T with speed v_T at time T. Furthermore, we wish to accomplish this in an economical fashion, that is, by using as little fuel as possible.
