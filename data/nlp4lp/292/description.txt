A farmer wants to manufacture a special plant nutrition using two types of fertilizers, A and B. Each kg of fertilizer A contains NitrogenA units of nitrogen, PhosphoricAcidA units of phosphoric acid, VitaminAA units of vitamin A, and VitaminDA units of vitamin D. Each kg of fertilizer B contains NitrogenB units of nitrogen, PhosphoricAcidB units of phosphoric acid, VitaminAB units of vitamin A, and VitaminDB units of vitamin D. The plant nutrition must meet a minimum requirement of MinNitrogen units of nitrogen and MinPhosphoricAcid units of phosphoric acid, and must not exceed MaxVitaminA units of vitamin A. The goal is to determine the amount of each fertilizer that minimizes the total amount of vitamin D in the nutrition.
