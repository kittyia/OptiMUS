A marketplace like Alibaba sells three SKUs in a single period. For each product, the retailer must choose exactly one retail price from a small list of candidate prices. For each product and candidate price, the demand is known in advance as a specific number of units. The retailer orders an integer quantity of each product from the wholesaler before demand is realized, and any unsold units at the end of the period are carried in inventory and incur a holding cost per unit. The wholesaler has a unit production cost for each product, and whenever the retailer decides to order a product at all, a fixed ordering cost is incurred for that product.

There are three products, labeled Product 1, Product 2, and Product 3. Product 1 can be priced at 10, 12, or 14. If Product 1 is priced at 10, the demand is 100 units; if priced at 12, the demand is 80 units; if priced at 14, the demand is 60 units. Product 2 can be priced at 8, 9, or 11. Its demand is 150 units at price 8, 120 units at price 9, and 90 units at price 11. Product 3 can be priced at 20, 22, or 25. Its demand is 70 units at price 20, 55 units at price 22, and 40 units at price 25.

The unit production costs are 6 for Product 1, 4 for Product 2, and 12 for Product 3. The fixed ordering costs are 200 for Product 1, 150 for Product 2, and 250 for Product 3. The unit holding costs are 0.5 for Product 1, 0.4 for Product 2, and 0.8 for Product 3. The total number of units ordered across all three products cannot exceed 250 units.

For each product, introduce a binary decision variable for each candidate price that indicates whether that price is chosen, an integer variable for the order quantity, and variables for the number of units actually sold at each candidate price. Total sales for a product are defined as the sum of the units sold at its chosen price. For every product, exactly one price must be selected. The number of units sold at the chosen price for any product cannot exceed the corresponding demand, and total sales of that product cannot exceed the quantity ordered. A binary variable indicates whether a product is ordered, and the order quantity is forced to be zero whenever this indicator is zero. The retailer also faces the overall limit that the sum of ordered units across the three products is at most 250. The objective is to maximize total channel profit, defined as total sales revenue minus production, fixed ordering, and holding costs.
