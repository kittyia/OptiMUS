A supermarket is planning promotion support for one coffee product over the next 6 weeks. The prices for the product have already been decided by a different team, so this problem only decides which promotion vehicles to use in each week.

The retailer can use three promotion vehicles: a flyer placement, an in-store display, and a local TV commercial. Using a vehicle in a week can increase that week’s profit because it makes customers more aware of the product and can boost demand.

If no promotion vehicle is used, the expected base profit for the 6 weeks is as follows: week 1 is 1200 dollars, week 2 is 1350 dollars, week 3 is 1280 dollars, week 4 is 1420 dollars, week 5 is 1500 dollars, and week 6 is 1380 dollars.

Each vehicle has a multiplicative boost factor for each week. If the flyer is used, it multiplies weekly profit by 1.10 in week 1, 1.08 in week 2, 1.12 in week 3, 1.09 in week 4, 1.11 in week 5, and 1.07 in week 6. If the display is used, it multiplies weekly profit by 1.06 in week 1, 1.09 in week 2, 1.07 in week 3, 1.10 in week 4, 1.08 in week 5, and 1.09 in week 6. If the TV commercial is used, it multiplies weekly profit by 1.15 in week 1, 1.13 in week 2, 1.12 in week 3, 1.14 in week 4, 1.16 in week 5, and 1.11 in week 6.

If more than one vehicle is used in the same week, their effects multiply. For example, if a week has a base profit of 1200 dollars and the flyer and display are both used in that week, then the boosted profit for that week becomes 1200 times 1.10 times 1.06.

The retailer cannot use vehicles without limits. Over the full 6-week horizon, the flyer can be used at most 2 times, the display can be used at most 3 times, and the TV commercial can be used at most 1 time.

There is also a weekly capacity rule. In weeks 1 through 6, the retailer can use at most 1, 2, 1, 2, 1, and 2 vehicles, respectively.

The decision is to choose, for each week, which of the three vehicles to assign. The goal is to maximize the total boosted profit over the 6 weeks while satisfying both the per-vehicle usage limits and the per-week capacity limits.
