- y_ip: type=continuous, desc=units of product i sold at price p
- y_i: type=continuous, desc=total units of product i sold
- z_i: type=binary, desc=1 if product i is ordered

OBJECTIVE:
- Sense: maximize
- Expression: ∑_i ∑_p Prices[i][p] * y_ip - ∑_i (c_i * Q_i + K_i * z_i + h_i * (Q_i - y_i))

- [one_price_per_product] ∑_p x_ip = 1 ∀ i
- [sales_definition] y_i = ∑_p y_ip ∀ i
- [sales_leq_demand] y_ip ≤ Demand[i][p] * x_ip ∀ i,p
- [sales_leq_inventory] y_i ≤ Q_i ∀ i
- [fixed_order_link] Q_i ≤ M_i * z_i ∀ i
- [capacity] ∑_i Q_i ≤ C
