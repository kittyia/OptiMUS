A platform needs to pick up to 5 phones out of 8 to feature on its homepage. The phones have these revenue and popularity numbers:

Phone 1: $12 revenue, popularity 3.0
Phone 2: $11 revenue, popularity 2.5
Phone 3: $10 revenue, popularity 2.2
Phone 4: $9 revenue, popularity 2.0
Phone 5: $8 revenue, popularity 1.8
Phone 6: $7 revenue, popularity 1.5
Phone 7: $6 revenue, popularity 1.2
Phone 8: $5 revenue, popularity 1.0

Each customer randomly picks one of the featured phones, with chances proportional to each phone's popularity relative to the sum of all featured phones' popularities plus 1.0 for the walk-away (no-buy) option. Which phones should the platform feature to get the highest expected revenue?
