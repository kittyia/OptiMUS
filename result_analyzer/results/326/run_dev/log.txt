### Why this is a constraint

The equation  
\[
\text{stock}_n = \text{stock}_{n-1} + \text{buyquantity}_n - \text{sellquantity}_n
\]
is the **inventory balance equation**. It enforces conservation of flow:

- Inventory carried from last period  
- Plus purchases in current period  
- Minus sales in current period  
- Equals ending inventory for the current period  

Without this constraint:

- Stock levels would not be linked across periods.
- The model could “sell” units that were never purchased.
- Capacity and holding costs would become meaningless.
- The requirement that only previously purchased stock can be sold would not be enforced.

This constraint is the structural backbone of any multi-period inventory LP.

- Initial condition:

  \text{stock}_N = 0

But the inventory balance equation itself is absolutely necessary.
