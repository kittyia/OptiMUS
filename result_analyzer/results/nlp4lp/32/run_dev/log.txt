The two constraints serve fundamentally different purposes:

1. **Demand satisfaction constraints** ensure that for each berry type (raspberries, blueberries, strawberries), the total production across all farms meets or exceeds the contractual requirement. These are functional constraints tied to fulfilling the contract.

2. **Non-negativity constraints** ensure that the decision variables (number of operating days for each farm) are physically meaningful. Negative operating days are not feasible, so these are domain constraints.

There is **no redundancy** between them:
- The demand constraints do not imply non-negativity.
- Non-negativity does not ensure demand satisfaction.
- They apply to different index sets (berries vs. farms).

They also cannot be meaningfully combined, since one applies per berry type and the other applies per farm.
